About Me

I'm a creative technologist, and being one is a lot like being a wedding planner. Clients come to me with grand visions, messy details, and ticking clocks (though thankfully fewer champagne flutes). I iron out the plan, separate the possible from the inadvisable, and get them to the launch chapel on time.

Some need help with the whole affair; others just the icing on the five-layer cake. So if you've got a product that needs finishing touches or a full-blown rescue, drop me a line. In-person meetings available in the DC Metro area.

  • Launchworks if you're a founder/"soloprenuer" needing help getting your product across the finish line.
  • Portfolio if you're looking for a long-term UX / front-end hire.
  • QuestRex if you're interested in my most excellent tabletop role-playing game.
